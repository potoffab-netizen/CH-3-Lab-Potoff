My Internship at Medtronic!

Welcome to my internship page! This summer, I interned at Medtronic. Here I will share what I learned during my time in Minneapolis.

This is what I did!

I was an intern on the Global Supply Management Team, Speficially the Responsible Supply Management Team! This was my first ever internship and honeslty I was so nervous! Through the 12 weeks, I gained confidnce, learned about myself, and initiated a global process.

I met many Senior Directors, made long lasting freinds, and met the CEO!

Geoff Martha: A Leader to Look Up To

Geoff Martha leads where he puts others first. He remebers people by name, listens to concerns, spearheaded the largest medtech aquisition in history, making Medtronic the largest medical device company in the world! I introduced myself to him when I saw him in the atrium, thanked him for his time and left inspired. The next day I went to work and he had found my email, written a personalized note, and wanted to meet for coffee! I was so excited, this has never happened to me before! Anyways we had coffee scheduled for the next day! We met for 40 minutes, that is around $7k! I left inspired and continued the connection thoughout summer. At the end, I wrote a hand written letter & got a nice succulent. He found my MSU email and sent me a personalized note, welcoming to the team, thanking me for the gift/note, and said he has the plant on his desk! I am so excited to be returning full time in Minneapolis!
