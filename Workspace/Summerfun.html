Missisipi River Views!

Minneapolis has many rivers, one of them being the Missisipi River!

This river has been critical to American history & trade within the country. Minneapolis is considered the "Mill City" with many old mills along the river. The river is also a great place to go for walks, and see the beautiful views of the city! The stone arch bridge is a pedestrian only bridge that is a wonderful expereince if you are in the area and wanting exceptional views! I highly reccomend going for a run or walk on this bridge!

Beautiful sights at Minnehaha Falls

Minnehaha Falls is a stunning natural wonder that draws visitors from around the world. The falls are a popular spot for hiking and photography, offering breathtaking views of the cascading water and surrounding landscape. Here is a photo I took when my family came to visit!

One of my favorite parts of the summer was going with family to Minnehaha Falls! It was so pretty seeing the nature, and they also had an art fair going on! I had a blast walking with my family & seeing pretty views.
